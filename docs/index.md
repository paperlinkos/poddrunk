## ⚡ Key Features
- **Counted Loop Repeat Engine**: Repeat any track or section exactly N times (1–99) before automatically moving forward.
- **5-Band Equalizer & Bass Boost**: Fine-tune frequencies with presets (Bass Boost, Vocal Clarity, Rock, Electronic, Flat) or custom settings.
- **Local Storage & Folder Browser**: Play MP3, FLAC, AAC, WAV, and OGG files directly from device storage or navigate folder trees.
- **Double-Tap Quick Seek**: Skip forward or backward by configurable intervals (10s, 15s, 30s, 60s).
- **Smart Sleep Timer**: Gentle auto-pause countdown or stop playback right when the track finishes.
- **100% Private & Offline**: No accounts, zero analytics or telemetry, and zero advertisements. MIT licensed.
